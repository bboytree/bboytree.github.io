Post-Lunch Wind-Down

Notes:

* a jacob sheep taking its well-earned nap

* these sheep are known for being piebald (unpigmented patches on a pigmented background) and multi-horned (usually between two and six)

* i met a city of london corporation volunteer, who knew all these sheep by name and identified them by their markings. this snoozing stunner is called Bow

* i learned that they are moved around different local parks throughout the year to mow the grass in a natural way, a.k.a. conservation grazing

* ram is the term for a male sheep, ewe for female
